Version: 0.18.0
Date: 2026-10-16
  Added:
    - A player standing somewhere they do not belong is put back on the surface this server
      owns. It happens only when they are not on that surface, and only after
      route_if_elsewhere has had its turn: looking at another server's planet is a journey
      and connect_to_server takes them there, and a redirect already in flight counts as
      handled, so nothing fights the connection being made.
    - A surface this server does not own is cleared on boot: every creature on it is
      destroyed and the chunks around the origin are deleted. A fleet server's save is a
      nauvis world whatever planet the server is for, so nauvis is generated -- spawn area
      and biters -- before anybody arrives, and on five of the six servers nobody ever goes
      there. Those biters are a UPS cost paid every tick by a server playing elsewhere.
    - The spawn point moves to the surface this server is for, so a player arriving without
      a body does not generate a fresh spawn area -- biters and all -- on a surface that was
      just cleared.
    - clear_surfaces, so the manager can ask for the same thing after loading a save.
  Changed:
    - The report says which surface the spawn point is on and what the last clear took.
