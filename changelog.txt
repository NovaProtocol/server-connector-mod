Version: 0.23.2
Date: 2026-10-17
  Fixed:
    - World changes happen only with nobody connected. They are driven by orders that arrive
      over RCON, and RCON-driven world changes are NOT replicated to clients: the server cut
      a surface down and the client, never told, kept it -- "Multiplayer desynchronisation:
      crc test failed" on join, every time. Clearing, the spawn move, the strip and the
      platform mirror all run empty now, and the boot-time work is re-armed when the last
      player leaves so a busy fleet still gets it done.

Version: 0.23.1
Date: 2026-10-17
  Fixed:
    - The strip step says what it does and what fails. It was called under pcall with nothing
      logged, so a strip that made no progress for eight minutes left every counter at zero
      and no word about why. The handler logs its error, a surface the job cannot find names
      itself, an empty surface says so, and a job with nothing to strip says that too.

Version: 0.23.0
Date: 2026-10-17
  Added:
    - Stripping a world is a job now, not one call. A 170MB world cannot be stripped inside
      an RCON round trip: the work outlives the timeout, the reply comes back empty, and an
      empty reply looks exactly like a refusal -- so the manager retried, the retry started
      the work again, and the world came out untouched while the conversion reported success.
      strip_begin starts it, a permanent on_tick handler does a batch every tick,
      strip_status says where it has got to, strip_finish returns the summary, and
      strip_cancel stops it. Every step is wrapped and says what it did.

Version: 0.18.0
Date: 2026-10-16
  Added:
    - A player standing somewhere they do not belong is put back on the surface this server
      owns. It happens only when they are not on that surface, and only after
      route_if_elsewhere has had its turn: looking at another server's planet is a journey
      and connect_to_server takes them there, and a redirect already in flight counts as
      handled, so nothing fights the connection being made.
    - A surface this server does not own is cleared on boot: every creature on it is
      destroyed and the chunks around the origin are deleted. A fleet server's save is a
      nauvis world whatever planet the server is for, so nauvis is generated -- spawn area
      and biters -- before anybody arrives, and on five of the six servers nobody ever goes
      there. Those biters are a UPS cost paid every tick by a server playing elsewhere.
    - The spawn point moves to the surface this server is for, so a player arriving without
      a body does not generate a fresh spawn area -- biters and all -- on a surface that was
      just cleared.
    - clear_surfaces, so the manager can ask for the same thing after loading a save.
  Changed:
    - The report says which surface the spawn point is on and what the last clear took.
